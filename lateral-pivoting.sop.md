# Lateral Pivoting Standard Operating Procedure

## Overview

Lateral pivoting is a strategic maneuver that enables teams to shift focus, resources, and capabilities from one domain or project to another with minimal disruption. This SOP establishes the framework for executing lateral pivots effectively, ensuring organizational agility while maintaining operational excellence.

The lateral pivot process is designed to:
- Facilitate rapid response to market changes
- Optimize resource allocation across competing priorities
- Enable knowledge transfer between teams and domains
- Maintain business continuity during strategic shifts

## Purpose

The primary purpose of this SOP is to provide a standardized approach for executing lateral pivots within the organization. This document serves to:

- Define clear criteria for when lateral pivoting should be initiated
- Establish decision-making processes for pivot approval
- Outline communication protocols for stakeholders
- Ensure minimal disruption to ongoing operations
- Capture lessons learned from each pivot initiative

## Scope

This SOP applies to:

### In Scope
- All product and engineering teams
- Cross-functional project initiatives
- Strategic business unit transitions
- Resource reallocation requests exceeding 20% of team capacity
- Market-driven strategic shifts requiring operational adjustment

### Out of Scope
- Individual task reassignments
- Minor scope adjustments within existing projects
- Temporary resource borrowing for specific sprints
- Personnel transfers unrelated to strategic pivots

## Objectives

The objectives of lateral pivoting are:

1. **Minimize Disruption**: Ensure that pivot execution causes minimal impact on customer-facing services and ongoing commitments
2. **Maximize Value Transfer**: Leverage existing capabilities, knowledge, and assets from the source domain for application in the target domain
